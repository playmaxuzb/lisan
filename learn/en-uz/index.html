LLisan · لِسان

Learn Uzbek

Lisan is a free platform for learning twelve languages across six levels (A1–C2): picture words, sentences and dialogues with clear audio, simple grammar, and an exam for every level.

Start learning
  • A1 · Beginner — From zero: letters, pronunciation, greetings and first everyday phrases.
  • A2 · Elementary — Shopping, travel and weather; talking about the past and the future.
  • B1 · Intermediate — Work, health and feelings, plus the core grammar patterns.
  • B2 · Upper-intermediate — Discussing and giving opinions on society, nature and the media.
  • C1 · Advanced — Formal style, articles and advanced linking words.
  • C2 · Proficient — Idioms, proverbs and the finest shades of style.
